Controls: a gamepad joins whole when you hold any of its buttons; hold one again and two players share it, the left half and the right half. The right half now bombs with A and uses its special with X. Every control set is drawn with its buttons marked, in Controls and when you choose a player's controls. The Controls screen scrolls to everything on a TV. Leaving a match goes back to the menus at once. New in Options: the frame rate and how long a press takes to reach the screen.
